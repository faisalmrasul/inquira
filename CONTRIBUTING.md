# Contributing to Inquira

Thank you for your interest in Inquira — The Trust Layer for Professional AI.
Inquira is a prototype that evaluates AI queries against the **EU AI Act
(Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)** as a universal design standard. We welcome
contributions that strengthen its compliance logic, UX, and documentation.

> ⚠️ This is a **prototype**. It is **not CE-marked** and makes **no legal
> compliance claim**. Contributions must preserve that disclaimer.
